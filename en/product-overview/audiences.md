# Target Users and Audiences

HiredChina provides differentiated services and support for users in different roles.

## Job seekers

For professionals seeking career opportunities at home and abroad.

**Core services**:
- Job search and application
- Resume upload and parsing
- Job recommendations
- Interview communication and application tracking
- AI customer service support

## Employers

For companies and organizations with hiring needs.

**Core services**:
- Job posting and management
- Resume screening and candidate communication
- Company profile building
- Data analytics and recruitment reports
- Talent search

## Headhunters

For professional headhunter partners.

**Core services**:
- HRS headhunter system
- Client and candidate management
- Job recommendations and commission settlement
- Recruitment progress tracking

## Growing together

No matter which user group you belong to, HiredChina is committed to providing a safe, efficient, and transparent recruitment experience.
