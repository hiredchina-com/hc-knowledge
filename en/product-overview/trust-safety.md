# Platform Trust and Safety

HiredChina is committed to building a safe and trustworthy recruitment environment.

## Company verification

- Employers must verify via company email or business credentials
- Headhunter partners must complete identity and qualification review
- Fraudulent companies will be banned and disclosed

## Information review

- Job postings are reviewed by machine and human moderators
- Users can report fake jobs or inappropriate behavior
- The platform promptly handles violations and accounts

## User protection

- Resume visibility is controlled by users
- Personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Important actions require two-factor verification
- Account anomaly login alerts are provided

## Fraud prevention tips

- Do not trust employers asking for upfront payment
- Do not share ID, bank card, or other sensitive information
- Confirm interview invitations through platform messages
- Report suspicious behavior immediately

## Reporting and feedback

- Click the "Report" button on job or message pages
- Email support@hiredchina.com
- Provide feedback via AI or human customer service
