# Premium Services

HiredChina offers a variety of premium services to help users achieve more efficient recruitment or job search results.

## Employer premium services

- **Job boosting**: Get higher exposure in search results
- **Priority resume viewing**: View newly submitted quality resumes first
- **Employer branding package**: Customize company profile and publish company updates
- **Recruitment consultant service**: One-on-one recruitment consultant support

## Job seeker premium services

- **Resume boosting**: Increase resume exposure in the talent pool
- **Career consultant consultation**: Get career planning advice
- **Interview coaching**: Professional mock interviews and feedback

## How to subscribe

1. Log in and go to "Premium Services" or "Membership Center"
2. Choose a suitable plan
3. Complete payment
4. Service takes effect immediately

## Notes

- Premium services are optional and do not affect basic features
- Specific services and prices are subject to the page display
- Invoices can be requested on the order page
