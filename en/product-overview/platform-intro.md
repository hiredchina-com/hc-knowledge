# HiredChina Platform Introduction

HiredChina is a comprehensive recruitment platform serving global Chinese and international talent, connecting job seekers, employers, and professional headhunters.

## Our mission

To help excellent talent and the right opportunities meet efficiently, no matter where they are.

## Platform highlights

- **Multilingual support**: Chinese, English, and other language interfaces for global users
- **Multiple roles**: Job seeker, employer, and headhunter roles to meet diverse needs
- **Intelligent matching**: AI-based job recommendations and talent search
- **Global coverage**: Covering mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, North America, Europe, and more
- **Professional services**: AI customer service, resume parsing, and recruitment management tools

## Who it is for

- **Job seekers**: Looking for quality job opportunities at home and abroad
- **Employers**: Posting jobs, managing candidates, and building employer brand
- **Headhunters**: Using the HRS system to efficiently serve clients and candidates

## Get started

1. Register and complete your personal or company profile
2. Search for jobs or talent
3. Submit applications or initiate conversations
4. Track progress and achieve your career goals
