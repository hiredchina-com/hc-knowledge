# Content and Events Guide

HiredChina's content and events section provides users with industry news, job fairs, and platform events.

## Browsing content

1. Go to the "News" or "Events" page
2. Filter by category: industry trends, career guides, policy updates, job fairs
3. Click an article title to view details
4. Bookmark articles of interest or share them with friends

## Participating in events

1. View upcoming events in the event list
2. Click "Register Now" and fill in the registration form
3. After successful registration, watch for email or site notifications
4. You will receive a reminder before the event starts

## Subscription

- Subscribe to content tags you are interested in
- Receive recommendations when new content is published
- Manage subscription frequency in "Notification Settings"

## Content feedback

- If you find inaccurate information, provide feedback at the bottom of the article
- Submit topic suggestions to the platform operations team
