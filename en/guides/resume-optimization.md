# Resume Optimization Guide

A great resume can make you stand out from other candidates. Here are HiredChina's resume optimization recommendations.

## Resume structure

1. **Personal information**
   - Name, phone, email, city
   - LinkedIn or portfolio link (optional)

2. **Career objective**
   - Target role, preferred location, availability

3. **Work experience**
   - List in reverse chronological order
   - Each role includes: company, position, dates, responsibilities, achievements
   - Quantify achievements, e.g., "Increased sales by 30%"

4. **Education**
   - School, major, degree, graduation year

5. **Skills and certifications**
   - Professional skills, languages, relevant certificates

## Optimization tips

- Keep your resume to 1-2 pages
- Use clean formatting and clear headings
- Highlight experience relevant to the target role
- Avoid spelling and grammar errors
- Tailor your resume for each application

## Optimizing on HiredChina

- Complete every section of your online resume
- Use the skill tags recommended by the system
- Regularly update work experience and job preferences
