# How do I write a good resume?

Your resume is the key to opening job opportunities. Follow these tips to make it more professional and appealing.

## Basic information

- Use your real name
- Provide a frequently used email and phone number
- State your current city

## Work experience

- Start with your most recent job
- Describe each role with 3-5 bullet points
- Emphasize achievements, not just responsibilities
- Examples:
  - Weak: Responsible for sales
  - Strong: Managed East China sales, acquired 20 new clients within 6 months, achieving 120% of quarterly target

## Education

- List your highest degree and related major
- Recent graduates can add courses, projects, or internships

## Skills section

- List hard skills relevant to the target role
- Indicate proficiency levels
- Avoid stacking irrelevant skills

## Common mistakes

- Resume too long or too short
- Typos
- Overly fancy templates
- False or exaggerated experience
