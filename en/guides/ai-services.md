# AI Services Guide

HiredChina integrates multiple AI services to help job seekers, employers, and headhunters improve efficiency.

## AI customer service

- Available 24/7 to answer common questions
- Supports Chinese, English, and other languages
- Can transfer to a human agent for complex issues

## AI resume parsing

- Automatically extracts key information after uploading a resume attachment
- Generates a structured online resume
- Supports PDF, DOC, and DOCX formats

## AI job recommendations

- Recommends jobs based on your profile, preferences, and behavior
- Recommendations improve as profile completeness increases
- View personalized recommendations on the "Recommended Jobs" page

## Usage tips

- A complete profile helps you get more accurate recommendations
- Resume parsing results should be manually reviewed
- For complex or sensitive issues, contact human customer service
