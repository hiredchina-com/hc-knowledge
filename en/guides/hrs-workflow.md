# HRS Headhunter System Workflow

HRS (Headhunter Resource System) is a professional recruitment management tool provided by HiredChina for headhunter partners.

## Main features

1. **Job management**
   - Receive job assignments from clients
   - Post jobs and manage candidate sources
   - Track recruitment progress for each job

2. **Candidate management**
   - Enter and edit candidate information
   - Tag candidate status and matched jobs
   - Manage candidate communication and interview scheduling

3. **Client management**
   - Maintain client company information
   - Record client requirements and feedback
   - Generate recruitment progress reports

4. **Commission and settlement**
   - View successful referral records
   - Track commission settlement status
   - Download settlement reports

## Quick start

1. Complete headhunter partner certification
2. Add or import candidates into the system
3. Match suitable jobs and make recommendations
4. Track interviews and hiring process
5. Apply for commission settlement after successful onboarding
