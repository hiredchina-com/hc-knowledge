# How do I search for talent?

Employers and headhunters can proactively discover qualified candidates through the talent search feature.

## Steps

1. Go to the "Talent Search" page
2. Enter keywords such as role, skills, or company name
3. Use filters:
   - Work location
   - Years of experience
   - Expected salary
   - Education
   - Language requirements
   - Resume update time
4. View the talent list and resume summaries
5. Initiate contact or send interview invitations to interested candidates

## Search result sorting

- Default sorting by relevance and activity
- Can switch to sort by update time
- More filters yield more precise results

## Communication best practices

- In the first message, introduce the role and company
- Respect candidate privacy settings
- Avoid sending irrelevant or harassing messages
- Respond promptly to candidate questions
