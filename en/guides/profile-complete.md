# How do I complete my profile?

A complete profile helps employers quickly understand your background and increases your chances of getting interviews.

## Steps

1. Log in and go to "Dashboard" → "My Profile"
2. Fill in the following information in order:
   - Basic info: name, gender, city, contact details
   - Job preferences: target role, preferred city, expected salary
   - Work experience: company, position, duration, main responsibilities
   - Education: school, major, degree, graduation date
   - Skills: languages, professional skills, certifications
3. Upload a clear profile photo
4. Click "Save"

## Profile completeness tips

- Profiles over 80% complete are more likely to be seen by employers
- Use quantified achievements when describing work experience
- Update your profile regularly to keep information accurate

## Common questions

- Profile information syncs to your online resume
- You can set some information to be visible only to employers you apply to
- Contact information is visible to verified employers by default
