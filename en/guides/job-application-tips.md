# Job Application Tips

Using the right application techniques can increase the chance that your resume gets viewed and responded to.

## Before applying

1. Read the job description carefully and confirm you meet the core requirements
2. Research the company and team background
3. Tailor your resume for the role
4. Prepare a concise cover letter

## When applying

- Choose the most suitable resume version
- In your cover letter, explain why you are a good fit
- Keep the cover letter short, under 200 words
- Double-check your contact information

## After applying

- Track status in "My Applications"
- If no response after 1-2 weeks, send a polite follow-up message
- Continue applying to other suitable positions

## Ways to improve success rate

- Prioritize roles that closely match your experience
- Stay active and update your profile regularly
- Complete your online resume to improve completeness
- Use the platform's job recommendation feature
