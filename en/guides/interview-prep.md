# Interview Preparation Guide

After receiving an interview invitation, thorough preparation helps you present your best self.

## Before the interview

1. Research the company's background, products, and culture
2. Review the job description and prepare relevant examples
3. Prepare a self-introduction of 1-2 minutes
4. Anticipate common questions and prepare answers
5. Confirm the interview time, format, and platform

## Common interview questions

- Tell me about yourself
- Why do you want to join our company?
- Talk about a project you are most proud of
- What is the biggest challenge you have faced?
- What are your career plans for the next 3 years?

## Video interview tips

- Test your camera, microphone, and network in advance
- Choose a quiet, well-lit environment
- Dress professionally
- Maintain eye contact and avoid distractions

## After the interview

- Send a thank-you message within 24 hours
- Summarize your performance and note areas for improvement
- Wait patiently for the employer's feedback
