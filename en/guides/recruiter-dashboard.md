# Recruiter Dashboard Guide

The recruiter dashboard is your central hub for managing jobs, reviewing candidates, and scheduling interviews.

## Main modules

1. **Job management**
   - Post new jobs
   - Edit published jobs
   - View job impressions and applications
   - Unpublish or republish jobs

2. **Candidate management**
   - View received resumes
   - Filter and tag candidates
   - Send interview invitations
   - Record interview feedback

3. **Company profile**
   - Edit company introduction and culture
   - Upload company logo and albums
   - Manage displayed open positions

4. **Analytics**
   - View recruitment funnel data
   - Analyze job exposure and conversion
   - Export recruitment reports

## Quick start

1. Complete employer verification with a company email
2. Complete your company profile
3. Post your first job
4. Set up team members and permissions
