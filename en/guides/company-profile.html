How do I set up a company profile?

A complete company profile enhances your employer brand and attracts more quality candidates.

Steps

1. Log in to your employer account and go to "Company Profile"

2. Click "Edit Profile"

3. Fill in company information:

- Company name and industry

- Company size and founding year

- Company address and website link

- Company introduction and culture

4. Upload company logo and cover image

5. Add company photos or office environment images

6. Save and publish

Optimization tips

  • Keep the company introduction within 300-500 words
  • Highlight corporate culture and employee benefits
  • Regularly update open positions
  • Respond to candidate inquiries to improve engagement

Common questions

  • The company profile is visible to all job seekers
  • Multiple admins can manage the profile
  • Changes to company information may require review before taking effect
