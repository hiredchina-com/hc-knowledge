# How to search and apply for jobs?

HiredChina offers multiple ways to search for jobs and submit applications, so you can find the right role quickly.

## Searching for jobs

1. After signing in, use the search bar at the top of the homepage
2. Enter a job title, company name, or keyword (e.g. "frontend engineer", "Shanghai")
3. Use filters to narrow results:
   - **Location**: city / remote
   - **Job type**: full-time / part-time / internship
   - **Experience**: entry-level / 1-3 years / 3-5 years / 5+ years
   - **Salary range**: filter by monthly salary band
4. Click a job card to open its full description

## Saving and subscribing

- Click the bookmark icon on a job card to save it to "My Favorites"
- Set keywords and location under "Job Preferences" to receive matching roles by email

## Submitting an application

1. On the job detail page, click "Apply"
2. Choose between your platform profile or upload a custom resume (PDF / DOCX)
3. Optionally add a short introduction (100-300 words recommended)
4. Click "Submit" to complete the application

## Tracking your applications

- View every submission and its status under "My Applications": applied / viewed / interview invited / not selected
- Companies typically respond within 3-7 business days
- If you haven't heard back, you can follow up via the "Messages" tab

## Job search tips

- Tailor your resume highlights to match each job description
- Adding a personal introduction within 24 hours of applying significantly improves response rates
- Prioritize quality over quantity — avoid mass-applying to dozens of roles in a single day
