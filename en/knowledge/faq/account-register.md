# How to create a HiredChina account?

Creating a HiredChina account takes just a few minutes and gives you access to job search, applications, and our AI career assistant.

## Registration steps

1. Visit the [HiredChina homepage](https://www.hiredchina.com/) and click "Sign Up" in the top-right corner
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a strong password (use uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters)
4. Fill in basic information: name, city, current role or job-seeking direction
5. Click "Send verification email"
6. Open your inbox and click the verification link to activate your account
7. Once signed in, complete your profile and start using the platform

## Third-party login

HiredChina also supports quick sign-in via:

- WeChat QR code
- Phone number + SMS code

These options create an account automatically on first sign-in — no separate registration needed.

## Common questions

- **Didn't receive the verification email?** Check your spam folder, or click "Resend"
- **Email already in use?** The email is already registered — go to the login page to reset your password
- **Is registration free?** Yes. HiredChina's core features are free; we never charge job seekers

## Security tips

- Use an email address you personally control — avoid temporary or shared accounts
- Keep your profile information accurate; false details may result in account restrictions
- Store your credentials safely and never share them with others
