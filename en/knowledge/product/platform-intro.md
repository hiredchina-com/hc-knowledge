# About HiredChina Platform

HiredChina is a multilingual job-seeking platform for candidates worldwide, using technology to make the job search faster and more effective.

## What we offer

- **Extensive job listings**: roles across tech, finance, manufacturing, education, and more — updated daily in major Chinese cities and beyond
- **Smart matching**: recommendations based on your resume and preferences
- **AI career assistant**: available 24/7 to answer questions about your account, resume, applications, and to provide interview tips
- **Direct employer chat**: connect with HR or hiring managers without intermediaries
- **Job-seeking tools**: resume templates, application tracker, interview calendar, salary insights

## Who it's for

- New graduates: start your career preparation from scratch
- Employed professionals: discreetly explore new opportunities
- Returnees / international candidates: bilingual Chinese-English support and cross-cultural guidance
- Freelancers / part-timers: flexible work opportunities

## Platform highlights

### Multilingual support

The interface and core documentation are available in both Chinese and English, so candidates from different backgrounds can use the platform comfortably.

### Public knowledge base

We publish frequently asked questions, usage guides, and product documentation as a [public knowledge base](https://knowledge-assets.hiredchina.com/) — readable even without an account.

### AI customer service integration

Knowledge base content feeds directly into our AI assistant. When you ask the assistant a question, it retrieves verified answers from the knowledge base first.
