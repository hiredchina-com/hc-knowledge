# How to write a good resume?

A strong resume is the first step toward landing an interview. Here is HiredChina's guide to writing one.

## Basic structure

A complete resume typically includes these sections:

1. **Personal info**: name, phone, email, city, LinkedIn / personal website (optional)
2. **Objective**: target role, preferred city, expected salary (a range is fine)
3. **Education**: school, major, degree, graduation year
4. **Work / internship experience**: reverse-chronological order, 3-5 bullet points per role
5. **Projects** (optional): highlight tech stack, your contribution, and outcomes
6. **Skills & certifications**: programming languages, tools, language test scores
7. **Summary** (optional): 2-3 sentences describing your core strengths

## Writing tips

### Quantify your impact

Avoid vague descriptions — show value with concrete results:

- ❌ "Managed the company's social media account"
- ✅ "Managed the official account; grew followers from 20K to 80K in 6 months and lifted average reads by 150%"

### Use the STAR method

Structure each bullet as:

- **S**ituation: the context you were working in
- **T**ask: what you were responsible for
- **A**ction: what you specifically did
- **R**esult: the outcome you delivered

### Tailor for each role

- Mirror keywords from the job description to pass ATS filters
- Use different resume versions for different roles — one size does not fit all
- Remove experience that isn't relevant to the target position

## Formatting and length

- Length: one page for new graduates; 1-2 pages for 3+ years of experience
- Fonts: use clean sans-serif (e.g. Helvetica, Calibri, or system Chinese fonts)
- Body text: 10-12pt; headings: 14-16pt
- Export as PDF before sending to avoid layout issues
- File name: `Name-Role-YearsExp.pdf` (e.g. `Jane-Smith-Frontend-Engineer-3.pdf`)

## Common mistakes

- Typos or grammar errors — proofread at least twice before submitting
- Using a selfie or casual photo — use a professional headshot or none at all
- Listing responsibilities without outcomes
- Generic summaries like "hardworking team player" — replace with specific, demonstrable strengths
