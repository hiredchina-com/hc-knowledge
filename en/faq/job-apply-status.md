# How do I check my application status?

After submitting an application, you can track its progress in your personal dashboard.

## Steps

1. Log in and click your profile photo to enter "Dashboard"
2. Select "My Applications" or "Application History"
3. View the status label for each application

## Status definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Submitted | Application successfully submitted, awaiting employer review |
| Viewed | Employer has viewed your resume |
| Contacting | Employer is interested and may contact you soon |
| Interview Invitation | Employer has invited you to an interview |
| Not a Fit | Employer decided the role does not match your background |
| Hired | Employer has extended an offer |

## Notes

- Different employers respond at different speeds; please be patient
- If the status has not updated for a long time, you may politely follow up via message
- Rejected applications do not affect your ability to apply for other jobs
