# How do I use the AI customer service?

HiredChina's AI customer service can help you quickly answer common questions and is available around the clock.

## Steps

1. Click the customer service Widget icon in the bottom-right corner of the page
2. Describe your issue in the input box
3. The AI will respond based on the knowledge base
4. If the issue is not resolved, you can choose to transfer to a human agent

## Questions suitable for AI customer service

- Account registration, login, password recovery
- Job search and application process
- Resume upload and editing
- Notification and privacy settings
- Platform feature instructions

## Tips for better answers

- Make your question specific, e.g., "How do I change my bound phone number?"
- If the answer is incomplete, provide more details and continue asking
- For account security or payment issues, we recommend transferring to a human agent
