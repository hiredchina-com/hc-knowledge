# How do I register for a HiredChina account?

Registering for a HiredChina account only takes a few minutes. You can sign up using your email or a third-party account.

## Steps

1. Visit the HiredChina homepage and click the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner
2. Choose your role: Job Seeker, Employer, or Headhunter
3. Enter a valid email address and set a password
4. Agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
5. Click "Sign Up Now"
6. Check your inbox for the verification email and click the activation link

## Did not receive the verification email?

- Check your Spam or Promotions folder
- Make sure the email address is spelled correctly
- Wait 1-2 minutes, then click "Resend verification email"
- If you still cannot receive it, try a different email or contact customer support

## Security tips

- Use a real and frequently used email address for account recovery
- Password should be at least 8 characters with letters and numbers
- Do not stay logged in on public devices
