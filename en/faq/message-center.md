# How do I use the Message Center?

The Message Center is your main channel for communicating with employers, headhunters, and platform support.

## Steps

1. Log in and click the "Messages" icon in the top navigation bar
2. View all conversations in the message list
3. Click a conversation to open the chat details
4. Type text, emojis, or attachments to reply

## Message types

- **Employer messages**: Communication about applications and interviews
- **System notifications**: Application status, account security, platform announcements
- **Support messages**: Replies from AI or human customer service

## Communication tips

- Be polite and professional
- Respond promptly to interview invitations and important notices
- Do not share sensitive information such as ID or bank card numbers
- Report suspicious messages to the platform
