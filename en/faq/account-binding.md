# How do I bind or change my phone number or email?

Binding your phone number and email improves account security and makes it easier to recover your account and receive important notifications.

## Steps

1. After logging in, click your profile photo and select "Account Settings"
2. In the "Contact Info" tab, click "Bind Phone" or "Bind Email"
3. Enter the phone number or email address you want to bind
4. Enter the verification code you receive
5. Click "Confirm Binding"

## Changing a bound contact

1. Go to "Account Settings" → "Contact Info"
2. Click "Change" next to the bound phone number or email
3. Verify your current binding (enter verification code or password)
4. Enter the new phone number or email and complete verification
5. Save the changes

## Common questions

- One phone number can only be bound to one HiredChina account
- After changing the binding, the old number/email will no longer receive account security notifications
- If you cannot verify the original binding, submit an identity verification request through customer support
