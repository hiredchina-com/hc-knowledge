# How is my resume privacy protected?

HiredChina values your personal information security and provides privacy settings to control who can see your resume.

## Privacy options

1. **Public resume**
   - All verified employers can find your resume
   - Suitable for users actively seeking more opportunities

2. **Visible to applied employers only**
   - Only employers for jobs you applied to can view your full resume
   - Suitable for cautious job seekers

3. **Fully private**
   - Other users and employers cannot find your resume
   - You can still actively apply for jobs

## How to set

1. Go to "Dashboard" → "Privacy Settings"
2. Select "Resume Visibility"
3. Save the settings

## Data security

- HiredChina does not sell your personal information to third parties
- Employers must comply with the platform privacy agreement to view your resume
- You can download or delete your resume data at any time
