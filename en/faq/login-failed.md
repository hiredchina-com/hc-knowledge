# What should I do if I cannot log in?

If you have trouble logging in to HiredChina, follow these steps to troubleshoot.

## Common causes and solutions

1. **Incorrect password**
   - Check whether Caps Lock is on
   - Click "Forgot password" to reset via email or phone

2. **Account not activated**
   - New accounts need to click the activation link in the verification email
   - If the email has expired, you can resend it

3. **Browser or network issues**
   - Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again
   - Try a different browser or network
   - Disable VPN or proxy and log in again

4. **Account temporarily locked**
   - Entering the wrong password too many times may temporarily lock your account
   - Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact customer support to unlock

## Still cannot log in?

- Make sure your email or phone number is spelled correctly
- Try logging in with an SMS verification code
- Contact support via the Widget on the homepage or email support@hiredchina.com
