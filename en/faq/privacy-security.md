# How does HiredChina protect my privacy and security?

HiredChina employs multiple technical and administrative measures to protect user data and account security.

## Privacy protection measures

- Resume visibility is controlled by you
- Personal information is encrypted in transit and at rest
- We do not sell or share data with unauthorized third parties
- Employers must be verified by the platform to view candidate information

## Account security measures

- Strong password policy support
- Login anomaly alerts
- Two-factor verification for important actions
- You can view recent login activity in "Account Settings"

## Fraud prevention reminders

- Do not transfer money or provide bank card information to any individual
- Confirm interview invitations through platform messages
- Be wary of employers asking for upfront fees
- Report suspicious behavior immediately

## What to do if your account is compromised

1. Try to reset your password via "Forgot password"
2. Check and update your bound phone number and email
3. Review recent login records and click "Sign out of all devices" if anything is suspicious
4. Contact customer support for further security handling
