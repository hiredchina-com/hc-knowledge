# How do I upload my resume?

Uploading a resume helps employers better understand your experience and increases your interview opportunities.

## Steps

1. Log in and go to "Dashboard" → "My Resume"
2. Click "Upload Resume Attachment"
3. Select your resume file from your computer (PDF, DOC, or DOCX)
4. Wait for the upload to complete
5. The system will automatically parse the content; you can also manually add missing information

## File requirements

- Format: PDF, DOC, DOCX
- Size: recommended under 10MB
- Content: include name, contact info, work experience, education, etc.
- Language: both Chinese and English resumes are accepted

## Resume parsing notes

- AI resume parsing extracts structured information as accurately as possible
- Please review and supplement any missing content
- If parsing is inaccurate, you can manually edit your online resume
