# How do I manage email notifications?

You can customize the types and frequency of email notifications HiredChina sends you.

## Steps

1. Log in and go to "Account Settings" → "Notification Settings"
2. Check or uncheck notification types under "Email Notifications"
3. Choose frequency: real-time, daily digest, or weekly digest
4. Save the settings

## Configurable notification types

- Application status updates
- New messages from employers
- Interview invitations
- Recommended jobs
- Important platform announcements
- Marketing emails (optional)

## Not receiving notifications?

- Check your spam or junk folder
- Add noreply@hiredchina.com to your whitelist
- Make sure the corresponding option is enabled in notification settings
- If your email has changed, update your bound email promptly
