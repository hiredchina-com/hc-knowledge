# How do I search and apply for jobs?

HiredChina offers multiple ways to help you find suitable jobs and submit applications quickly.

## Searching for jobs

1. Enter job keywords, company name, or location in the homepage search box
2. Use filters: location, industry, salary range, experience, language requirements, etc.
3. Click "Search" to view the results
4. Switch between "Most Recent" and "Most Relevant" sorting
5. Click a job card to view details

## Applying for a job

1. Open the job detail page and read the description and requirements carefully
2. Click the "Apply Now" button
3. Select the resume you want to submit (online resume or attachment)
4. Fill in a cover letter (optional but recommended)
5. Submit the application after confirming the information

## Tracking your application

- View all submitted applications on the "My Applications" page
- Statuses include: Submitted, Viewed, Contacting, Interview Invitation, Not a Fit, Hired
- You will receive email and site notifications when an employer responds
